Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 7, 2026

Owens Flooring Co. ("Owens Flooring," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through this website (the "Site"), how we use it, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information that you choose to provide to us directly through the forms on this Site:

  • Quote Request Form — name, company name (optional), email address, phone number, service requested, approximate project square footage, and any project details you include in your message.
  • Installer Interest Form — name, phone number, email address, experience level, years of experience, types of flooring you have installed, and any additional information you choose to share.

We do not currently use cookies, analytics, or advertising tracking technology on this Site. If that changes in the future, we will update this Policy.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you submit only to:

  • Respond to your request for a flooring estimate or quote
  • Evaluate installer interest submissions and follow up about potential work
  • Communicate with you about your inquiry
  • Maintain our own business records

We do not use your information for advertising and we do not sell it.

3. How Your Information Is Shared

Forms on this Site are processed using Formspree, a third-party form-processing service. When you submit a form, your information is transmitted to and stored by Formspree on our behalf so that we can receive and respond to it. Formspree's handling of your data is governed by its own policy, available at formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy.

Other than through Formspree, we do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties, except:

  • As required by law, subpoena, or other legal process
  • To protect the rights, property, or safety of Owens Flooring Co., our customers, or others

4. Data Retention

We keep the information submitted through our forms for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry and for our internal business and record-keeping purposes, unless you ask us to delete it sooner.

5. Children's Privacy

This Site is intended for business and commercial use and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under the age of 13.

6. Your Choices & Privacy Rights

You may contact us at any time to ask what information we have collected about you, request that we correct or delete it, or opt out of future communications. This applies regardless of which state you live in. To make a request, use the contact information below.

7. Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect information submitted to us. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time — for example, if we add new features, forms, or tracking technology to the Site. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
